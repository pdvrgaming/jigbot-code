Engine Architecture

The Adaptive Grid Advantage

Most trading bots fail because they force rigid orders onto changing market volatility. Jig Bot acts as an intelligent adapter, dynamically adjusting order spacing and volume weighting.

1. Multi-Bar Momentum Filter

Evaluates consecutive M1 candle momentum to filter market noise, ensuring the initial position triggers only during sharp directional exhaustion points.

2. Staggered Cluster Recovery

Rather than dumping massive risk at once, adverse moves trigger a 3-order execution cluster spaced with sub-second timing to simulate natural institutional order flow.

3. Volume-Weighted (VWAP) Exit

Discards fixed individual stops. Automatically synthesizes all active cluster positions into a unified volume-weighted price line to exit the entire basket cleanly in profit.

4. Strict Single-Direction Lock

Prevents conflicting bi-directional exposure. The bot locks into the active basket until resolution, shielding equity against unpredictable whipsaws.

Quick Setup Guide

Deploy on MetaTrader 5 in 3 Minutes

The bot is distributed as a compiled, secured .ex5 binary file. Follow these simple steps to install it on your MT5 terminal:

1

Download Binary

Download jig_bot.ex5 via Google Drive. Pure compiled binary with zero code leaks.

2

Place in MT5 Folder

In MT5, go to File → Open Data Folder. Paste .ex5 into MQL5 \ Experts, and the audio files into Sounds.

3

Attach to XAUUSD M1

Open an XAUUSD M1 chart. Refresh Navigator, drag the bot to chart, enable "Allow Algo Trading". Note your Account ID on the HUD.

4

Enter License Key

Request your key from Razel Tech. Press F7 on your chart, paste into InpLicenseKey, and hit OK to activate!
